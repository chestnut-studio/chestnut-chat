## Design Context

### Users

Chestnut Chat serves people working through extended AI conversations, often with technical or detailed content. They need to stay focused on the current answer while quickly locating and revisiting earlier questions.

### Brand Personality

Calm, precise, and efficient. The interface should feel dependable and unobtrusive, with motion used to clarify relationships rather than attract attention to itself.

### Aesthetic Direction

Refined minimalism inspired by DeepSeek's chat experience: neutral surfaces, compact controls, theme-aware cyan accents, strong content focus, and progressive disclosure. Support both light and dark themes, user-configurable color and radius tokens, keyboard navigation, and reduced motion. Avoid decorative gradients, bouncy motion, oversized controls, and heavy card nesting.

### Design Principles

1. Keep conversation content visually dominant; secondary navigation stays quiet until requested.
2. Reveal contextual controls with compact, anchored popover-like motion.
3. Reuse Nuxt UI semantic tokens so customization and both color modes remain coherent.
4. Prefer fast, transform-and-opacity motion with reduced-motion fallbacks.
5. Preserve accessible focus states, clear active states, and forgiving interaction targets.
